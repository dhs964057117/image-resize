How to Add Favicons to Your Website

After generating your favicon bundle, extract the files to your website's root public directory and paste these HTML tags into your <head> section:

<!-- Standard Web Favicons --> <link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="32x32" href="/favicon-32x32.png"> <link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="16x16" href="/favicon-16x16.png"> <!-- iOS / macOS Touch Icon --> <link rel="apple-touch-icon" sizes="180x180" href="/apple-touch-icon.png"> <!-- Android / PWA Manifest --> <link rel="manifest" href="/site.webmanifest">
