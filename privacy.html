Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our core principle. Here is how CutImage handles your data and respects your rights.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing (Zero Uploads)

CutImage operates entirely within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, JavaScript, and WebAssembly technologies. We do not upload, transmit, store, or view any of your images or files on our servers.

All image modifications (compressing, converting, cropping, resizing, favicon generating, and vector tracing) happen locally in your device's memory. When you close or refresh the browser tab, all temporary memory buffers are instantly purged.

2. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements when you visit our website in order to keep our image tools 100% free for everyone.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

3. Cookies and Web Storage

CutImage itself does not set first-party cookies for accounts or tracking. Cookies may be placed by Google and third-party advertising partners to measure advertising effectiveness and prevent ad fraud, in accordance with applicable data privacy laws.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights regarding their personal information. Because we do not collect personal identities or store your uploaded image files, we do not sell or monetize personal customer datasets.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of CutImage constitutes agreement to the updated policy.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please consult our FAQ page.
