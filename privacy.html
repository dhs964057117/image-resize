Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how CutImage handles your data.

1. No Data Collection

CutImage processes all images entirely in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server. We do not collect, store, or transmit your images, personal data, or usage patterns.

This website is designed as a privacy-first tool. Every image processing operation — compression, conversion, cropping, resizing, tracing, and favicon generation — happens locally using WebAssembly and Canvas APIs.

2. No Cookies

CutImage does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any form of persistent storage for analytics or advertising purposes. We do not use third-party analytics services.

3. Local Storage

We do not use localStorage or IndexedDB for storing your data. Any settings you configure during a session (such as quality sliders or format selections) exist only in memory and are lost when you close the page.

4. Third-Party Services

This website loads Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans) from Google's CDN. Your browser may make a request to Google's servers to fetch these fonts. No other third-party resources are loaded.

The ESRGAN upscaling model is loaded from the site's own server and processed entirely client-side via TensorFlow.js. No data is sent to external AI services.

5. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of CutImage after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through our FAQ page.
