Why Vectorize Your Graphics?

Raster images (JPG, PNG) store colors as fixed pixel grids. When scaled up, they become blurry and pixelated. By converting bitmap images into SVG paths, equations define curves and polygons, producing crystal-clear visuals at any resolution.
