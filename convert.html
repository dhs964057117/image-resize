Which Image Format Should You Choose?

WebP / AVIF

Best for modern websites. Yields 30-50% smaller sizes than JPG and PNG with full transparency support.

PNG

Best for graphics with sharp edges, logos, screenshots, and artwork requiring alpha channel transparency.

JPEG

Best for photographic images on legacy platforms and email templates where universal compatibility is essential.
